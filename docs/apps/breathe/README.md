# Breathe

Breathe to a rhythm, while the lights and a soft sound rise and fall with you, until you
stop.

- **Wheel**: choose the rhythm:
  - **Coherence**: breathe in for 5 seconds, out for 5, six breaths a minute.
  - **4-7-8**: breathe in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8, to fall asleep.
  - **Square**: breathe in, hold, breathe out, hold, 4 seconds each.
- The screen draws one breath of the chosen rhythm: this is what the lights will do.
- **Yellow**: start.

## Breathing

- Both lights rise as you breathe in, stay on while you hold, go down as you breathe
  out, and stay off while you hold again.
- A sound like the sea rises and falls with each breath in and each breath out. It is
  silent while you hold.
- The screen shows how long you have been breathing, in minutes.
- **Wheel**: the volume, kept for next time.
- **Yellow**: stop.

## Configuration

- `cute-display/apps/breathe/breathe.conf`: the rhythm and the volume. The device writes
  it. Without a card, they are kept only until the power goes off.
