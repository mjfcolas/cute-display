# Breathe

Breathing to a rhythm, the lights and a breath of sound rising and falling with you,
until you stop.

- **Wheel**: the rhythm:
  - **Coherence**: in for 5 seconds, out for 5, six breaths a minute.
  - **4-7-8**: in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8, to fall asleep to.
  - **Square**: in, hold, out, hold, 4 seconds each.
- The screen draws one breath of the rhythm chosen: what the lights will do.
- **Yellow**: start.

## Breathing

- Both lights rise as you breathe in, stay up while you hold, fall as you breathe out,
  and stay out while you hold again.
- A sound like the sea rises and falls with each breath in and each breath out, and is
  silent while you hold.
- The screen says how long you have been breathing, by the minute.
- **Wheel**: the volume, kept for next time.
- **Yellow**: stop.

## Configuration

- `cute-display/apps/breathe/breathe.conf`: the rhythm and the volume, written by the
  device. Without a card, they last until the power goes.
