# Updating

What to know before updating, under the version it comes with: what an update loses or
changes for whoever owns the clock, not all it changes, which is the
[changelog](CHANGELOG.md)'s. The installer shows the entries after the installed version
up to the one it installs, before it updates.

## Unreleased

- The alarm's wake-up times, and whether it is on, are lost: set them again on the clock.
- The place, the time zone and the airports the radar names are lost: answer the setup's
  questions again after the update, or the weather, the alarm clock's forecast and the
  radar show nothing, and the time is Central European.
- Update with the [one line](docs/install.md#in-short), not an installer you already
  had: one from 2026.9.1 or before neither shows this nor writes the new settings.
