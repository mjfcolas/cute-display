# Updating

What to know before you update, under the version that brings it. It lists only what an
update loses or changes for people who own the clock. Everything else that changes is in
the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). Before it updates, the installer shows the entries after
the installed version, up to the version it installs.

## 2026.10.1

- Zen, the timer and Breathe are new apps. They run only if you choose them: if you
  chose your apps before, answer the setup's question again.

## 2026.10.0

- The alarm's wake-up times, and whether the alarm is on, are lost: set them again on
  the clock.
- The place, the time zone and the airports that the radar names are lost: answer the
  setup's questions again after the update. If you do not, the weather, the alarm
  clock's forecast and the radar show nothing, and the time is Central European Time.
- Update with the [one line](docs/install.md#in-short), not with an installer you
  already have: an installer from 2026.9.1 or before does not show this, and does not
  write the new settings.
