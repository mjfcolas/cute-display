# Changelog

What changes from one release to the next, for whoever owns the clock.
[Releasing](docs/releasing.md) says how one is made.

## Unreleased

- [Zen](docs/apps/zen/README.md): a sound to relax or fall asleep to, for 1 to 12 hours,
  every day at a time or whenever you start it, the lights dimming; brown noise, or
  Habity's sleep sounds, which the setup offers to copy.
- Alarm ringtones may be WAV files too.
- [Timer](docs/apps/timer/README.md): a countdown that rings, pomodoro rounds of work and
  breaks that chime, or a nap that wakes you with the light, then a ringing.
- [Breathe](docs/apps/breathe/README.md): breathing to a rhythm, cardiac coherence, 4-7-8
  or square, the lights and a breath of sound rising and falling with you until you stop.
- The system app's apps scroll when they do not all fit, an arrow saying more are above
  or below.
- A change across most of the screen, such as going from one app to another, shows in
  one go, twice as fast.
- The setup copies Habity's sounds about four times faster, and the SD card is read from
  the computer seven times faster: Habity's card, about 300 MB, in a quarter of an hour
  instead of two hours.
- The card, reached from the computer, answers its first request too: after the clock
  had been left alone a while, the first one often got "no answer from the device".
- The chosen alarm ringtone plays: often the chime played instead, or the same short beep
  again and again. Files read from or written to the card were sometimes wrong in the
  same way; this is fixed too.

## 2026.10.0

- Alarm ringtones: Habity's own beside the chime, chosen in the alarm's settings, each
  playing softly while the wheel goes round them; the setup offers to copy them from
  Habity's sounds on the card.
- Choose which apps run, and in which order: the setup asks.
- A change made while the screen was drawing, such as the time set by the network,
  sometimes waited a minute to show; it now shows at once.
- Before an update, the setup says what to [know before updating](UPDATING.md); an
  installer too old for a release says so and installs nothing.
- Update with the [one line](docs/install.md#in-short), not an installer you already
  had: one from 2026.9.1 or before neither shows what to know nor writes the new settings.

## 2026.9.1

- One line installs the installer and runs the setup, step by step: the backup, Cute
  Display, then its settings; on Windows, in the Command Prompt.

## 2026.9.0

- Apps: [system](docs/apps/system/README.md), [alarm clock](docs/apps/alarm/README.md),
  [weather](docs/apps/weather/README.md), [radar](docs/apps/radar/README.md).
- The SD card served on the USB cable: the [maintenance console](docs/maintenance/README.md).
- Installed beside Habity's firmware, which stays bootable.
